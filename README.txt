WHAT THIS IS
----------------------------------------------------------------

A single-file web application that supports a university committee
through the revision of its vision and mission statements.

It does not write the statements. It holds the drafting to the
criteria that the scholarly literature supports, records the
committee's decisions, and shows where a draft is still weak.

Everything runs inside the browser. There is no account, no
installation, no server, and no data ever leaves the user's
device.

HOW TO START
----------------------------------------------------------------

1.  Open the HTML file in a browser, or visit the address above.
    Chrome or Edge is recommended (see BROWSER SUPPORT below).

2.  Press "Load a sample file" in the top bar. Every field fills
    with a complete worked example — a proposed vision, three
    mission lines and the Mechanical Engineering cascade. The
    scorecard is deliberately left unscored: scoring the sample
    yourself on the Evaluate tab is the best way to learn the
    rubric.
    On the Overview tab, "The seven-stage process — view"
    expands the recommended way to run the whole exercise
    (mandate map, evidence, diagnosis, consultation, drafting,
    cascade, adoption), each stage with its participants,
    output and gate, and indicative weeks on a twenty-week
    default span.

3.  Work the tabs in order. They are numbered because the
    sequence matters: Vision, then Missions, then Cascade, then
    Evaluate. The grey text inside each empty box is a
    suggestion; it disappears as soon as you type. Live warnings
    appear as you write: stock phrases ("centre of excellence",
    "quality education"), missing research, ranking language and
    broken word ceilings are flagged at the point of typing.

4.  Press Save, or Ctrl+S, and choose where to keep your file.
    After that the toolkit saves silently every 30 seconds and
    on every subsequent Save — no dialog. That one file holds
    everything and can be e-mailed to a colleague.

5.  On the Evaluate tab, press "Copy full record for
    documentation" and paste into Word. That is the committee
    paper: existing statements, the proposal, the mapping, the
    scores and the verdict.


----------------------------------------------------------------
THE TABS
----------------------------------------------------------------

01 VISION
    One sentence, settled before anything else. Record the core
    ideology that will not change, the envisioned future, and an
    internal planning horizon — no year is written into the
    published sentence. Then break that future into two to four
    STRANDS: the specific things that must become true.

02 MISSIONS
    Fill the five components (purpose, whom AUST serves,
    offerings, scope, distinctive capability), then compose the
    three mission lines M1, M2 and M3 in the form AUST already
    publishes. Under each line, tick the strands it advances.
    The coverage panel warns when a strand has no mission behind
    it, or a mission advances no strand.

03 CASCADE
    Accreditation never audits the institutional mission on its
    own; it checks whether each programme's educational
    objectives follow from it. Test one programme here: enter
    its vision, missions and PEOs, and tick which mission line
    each derives from. The verdict box turns green when the
    cascade holds and red when it does not. A worked Mechanical
    Engineering case loads with one click.

04 EVALUATE
    Eleven criteria scored 1 (absent) to 4 (strong), with AUST's
    current statements in a locked column beside the draft so
    the improvement is visible as a number out of 44. "Apply the
    automatic checks" scores up to nine criteria automatically,
    each with its reason shown, for the committee to confirm or
    override. Four criteria are marked ESSENTIAL: if any scores
    below 3, the draft is revised rather than debated.

    The thresholds are stated below the verdict: 35-44
    adoption-ready, 26-34 workable, below 26 revise -- and the
    essential criteria override the total. A sticky summary
    keeps the running total and verdict in view while scrolling,
    and "Copy full record for documentation" produces the
    committee paper.

05 KNOWLEDGE BASE
    Every term explained in plain language with the scholarship
    behind it — vision versus mission, institutional
    isomorphism, the accreditation cascade, graduate attributes,
    decoupling, why statements drift into boilerplate. Includes
    a group on the Bangladesh regulatory fixed points: the
    Private University Act 2010, the UGC Strategic Plan
    2018-2030, the BNQF, BAC/IQAC, BAETE under the Washington
    Accord, and a note on publishing in Bangla. Fifteen minutes
    here before the first meeting puts every member on the same
    footing.

06 REFERENCES
    The full reference list, grouped by the job each source
    does, with a note on citation integrity.


----------------------------------------------------------------
SAVING AND SHARING
----------------------------------------------------------------

Save / Ctrl+S      Writes one small file containing everything:
                   drafts, mappings and scores. Silent after the
                   first time.
Open...            Loads a saved file back.
Reconnect          Beside the app name; reopens the last file
                   used, in one click, even after a reload.
Load sample        Fills every field with the worked example.

Each tab also has a red "copy for sharing" button that places
its contents on the clipboard as plain text, for e-mail,
WhatsApp or Word. Recipients need nothing installed.

AI PROMPT BUTTONS (Vision and Missions tabs) assemble a prompt
from what has been written together with every constraint — word
ceilings, the facts only AUST can claim, the vocabulary to
avoid. Paste it into any AI tool to obtain alternatives to react
to. The prompt is deliberately a constrained rewriter of the
committee's own material, not a generator of new statements:
asked cold, a language model produces precisely the
interchangeable text this exercise exists to avoid.


----------------------------------------------------------------
BROWSER SUPPORT
----------------------------------------------------------------

Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers
    Full support. Save writes directly back to the same file,
    silently, and auto-save works. Reconnect restores the file
    handle after a reload (one permission click).

Safari and Firefox
    Everything works except direct file writing, which those
    browsers do not permit. Save produces a download instead,
    and Reconnect asks you to re-select the file. Use Open... to
    load a saved file.

Mobile
    Fully responsive. Tabs wrap into a grid; all text boxes grow
    to fit their content so nothing is hidden.


----------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE TOOLKIT WILL NOT DO
----------------------------------------------------------------

Three of the eleven criteria cannot be checked automatically,
and the toolkit says so rather than guessing:

  - Mandate and cascade alignment beyond the single programme
    tested, which needs written confirmation from accreditation
    coordinators;
  - Decision-filter power — whether the statement can be
    invoked to say no to a proposal;
  - Process legitimacy — whether the consultation actually
    reached students, alumni, junior faculty and employers.

The word ceilings (vision 25 words, mission lines 30 words) are
craft conventions consistent with the conciseness evidence, not
empirically derived cut-offs. The committee may adjust them.

No candidate wording is imposed. The suggestions and worked
examples are starting points to argue with.


----------------------------------------------------------------
BASIS
----------------------------------------------------------------

Every criterion, process step and failure mode in the toolkit is
drawn from a scholarly literature review prepared for the AUST
committee. Each of the eleven criteria carries its grounding on
screen; the References tab lists all sources, grouped by use.
Page ranges, issue numbers and DOIs should be confirmed against
Scopus, Web of Science or the publisher of record before any
reference is reproduced in a formal committee or accreditation
document.
